# OpenCV AI Competition 2026 alignment

## Mandatory
- OpenCV 5 performs substantive image/video analysis.
- Meaningful component runs on AWS.
- Technical report: problem, users, architecture, OpenCV 5, AWS, evaluation, limitations, responsible use.
- Judge-accessible repository/archive with pinned dependencies and build/deploy/test instructions.
- Architecture diagram.
- Working endpoint or arranged live demo.
- <=5 minute judge-accessible video showing team, working app, architecture, principal results.
- Evaluation evidence including failures/limitations.

- AWS/reproducibility/responsible operation 10%
